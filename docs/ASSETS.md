## v37.2.0 procedural art notes

The battlefield renderer follows the 18x32 authoritative grid. River, two bridges, 3x3 side-tower pads and the 4x4 core pad align with the same cell geometry used by combat and placement logic. Unit/card artwork remains procedural and smooth-moving; sprites are not restricted to tile centres.

- v37.2 adds five visual terrain themes and themed non-colliding border decoration; no external image assets are required.
