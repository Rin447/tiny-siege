# Original procedural art, v5

All in-game unit drawings are generated by the project's own Canvas vector/cut-out code; no external character art is loaded at runtime.

v5 adds simplified game-ready front/back designs for:

- ルミナ司祭: ivory robe, gold halo/staff, blue healing core.
- フロストシャーマン: fur hood, ice crystal staff, cyan frost accents.
- ストームハーピー: blue storm wings, airborne silhouette, yellow lightning accents.

The existing Iron Boar art is retained; its v5 change is physical behavior rather than a redesign.

`docs/previews-v5/` contains local Chromium render checks. These are not Cloudflare production screenshots.
